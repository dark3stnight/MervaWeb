How we keep your data secure
AES-256 encryption

Every piece of financial data — names, amounts, currencies, and categories — is encrypted with AES-256-CBC before being stored. A unique random initialization vector is generated for each value, so no two ciphertexts are alike even for identical inputs.

No account required

Mervane never asks for your name, email, or password. Your identity is represented solely by a randomly generated access token stored in your browser. No personally identifiable information is ever collected or stored.

Token-based access

Your token is looked up using a one-way SHA-256 hash — the raw token value is never transmitted during authentication. Keep your token private; anyone who has it can access your data.
